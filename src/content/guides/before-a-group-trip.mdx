The trip ends, the tab stays. Often the problem is not the amount itself; it is that nobody talked about who would pay for what. Spend five minutes before you leave on the points below. This is not financial advice, just a way to keep things clear between friends.

## 1. What is everyone roughly planning to spend?

You do not have to name a number, but "keep it cheap" and "let's get a nice place" are not the same thing. Talk about a rough expectation for lodging and food so nobody is surprised later.

## 2. A shared pot or paying one by one?

There are two ways. With a pot, everyone puts in an amount up front and small costs come out of it. Paying one by one means whoever paid is recorded and you settle up at the end. Either works; what matters is that the group knows which one it picked.

## 3. Who pays for what?

Fuel, hotel, groceries, tickets. For a few big items, say who will take care of them. If one person keeps pulling out their card, keeping a record takes the weight off them.

## 4. What is not shared?

If one person wants an expensive dinner or a spa visit, it does not have to land on everyone's tab. Decide up front which costs are split only between the people involved.

## 5. When and where do you write expenses down?

Instead of trying to remember on the drive home, log each expense when it happens. In Orti you can create a group so everyone has one place: who paid, who shares it and how much stays in the record.
