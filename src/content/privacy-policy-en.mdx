## 3. What Personal Data Do We Collect?

To deliver our core expense-sharing, balance optimization, and financial tracking features, we process the following categories of data:

### 3.1. Account and Identity Data
* **Email Address:** Used for passwordless authentication (Magic Link), account verification, and critical service notifications.
* **Display Name / Profile Alias:** The name or nickname you choose so members of your groups and friend lists can identify you.
* **Profile Picture (Optional):** An optional avatar image selected by you to personalize your profile.
* **User Identifier (UID):** A randomly generated, unique internal identifier representing your account securely across our services.
* **Sign in with Google:** If you sign in with Google, we receive the name, email address, and profile photo link from your Google account. If your account has no profile photo yet, this link may be used as your profile photo.
* **Sign in with Apple:** If you sign in with Apple, we receive your email address. If you choose "Hide My Email", this is a private relay address generated by Apple, and our emails are delivered to it. Your name is received only to the extent Apple allows you to share it at first sign-in.
* **No passwords received:** When you sign in with Google or Apple, your password is never sent to us. The identity token issued by these providers is verified through Firebase solely to authenticate you.

### 3.2. Group, Ledger, and Expense Records
* **Groups and Friend Lists:** Group names, types (e.g., home, trip, couple, project), member lists, and invitation statuses.
* **Transaction Records:** Expense title, amount, currency, transaction date, expense category, payer identity, and split distribution breakdown (equal, exact amounts, percentages, or custom shares).
* **Balances and Settlement History:** Net bilateral debts and receivables, settlement logs indicating mutual payments between parties.
* **Shared Notes and Goals:** Group savings goals, contribution entries, and shared text notes.

### 3.3. Receipts and Invoice Images
* **User-Uploaded Documentation:** Photos of receipts, bills, or invoices selected from your camera or photo gallery to attach to specific expense entries.
* **Scanned Text Data:** Line items, totals, dates, and merchant names recognized via on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR).

### 3.4. Device, Session, and Technical Data
* **Session Logs:** IP addresses, login timestamps, accessed API endpoints, and authentication tokens.
* **Device Characteristics:** Operating system and version (iOS / Android), device hardware model, app version, and system language settings.
* **Push Notification Token:** A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for your device, used to deliver push notifications. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

### 3.5. Diagnostics and Crash Reports
* Technical crash logs (stack traces), device memory/battery state at the time of failure, and anonymous installation identifiers processed via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve crashes and maintain stability.

### 3.6. Support Communications
* Information provided when you contact our support desk via email, including message contents and inquiry logs.

## 4. Data We Explicitly DO NOT Collect

As part of our privacy-first design, Orti **never** collects or processes:

* **Bank Account and Credit Card Numbers:** Orti is not a bank, payment service provider, or electronic money institution. No actual financial transactions or money transfers occur within the app. Credit card numbers, expiration dates, CVVs, or online banking passwords are never requested or stored.
* **Phone Contacts / Address Book:** We do not read, scan, or upload your device's contacts list.
* **Precise GPS Location:** We do not track or record your background or real-time GPS location.
* **Biometric Data:** Fingerprints, facial geometry, or voice recordings are never collected or stored.
* **Special Categories of Sensitive Data:** We do not process health, racial, ethnic, political, or religious data. We ask users not to input such data into expense notes or group titles.

Our app requests camera or gallery access only upon explicit user action:

These permissions are never accessed in the background. You can deny camera or gallery permissions at any time in device settings; you will still be able to input expenses manually.

## 6. On-Device Receipt Scanning (OCR) and AI Privacy

Receipt scanning is performed using Google ML Kit Text Recognition **entirely on your local device (on-device processing)**:
* Raw camera and receipt image data is analyzed locally on your smartphone and is **not transmitted** to third-party AI cloud services for processing.
* The receipt image is not sent to our servers during scanning. It is uploaded only when you **save** the expense, as an attachment to that expense; if you discard the expense, it is not uploaded.
* Google ML Kit may occasionally contact Google servers for bug fixes and model updates and may send API performance and usage metrics to Google; your receipt content is not part of these metrics.
* We recommend ensuring that receipts do not expose sensitive third-party details or payment card numbers prior to saving.

We only share your data with the following specific categories of recipients necessary for providing our services:

1. **Other Members of Your Groups and Friends:**
   * Group members can see your display name, avatar, expenses you add, receipts, notes, savings goals, and mutual settlement balances.
   * This sharing is the fundamental function of the collaborative expense tracking service.

2. **Cloud Infrastructure and Database Providers:**
   * Our application servers, databases, and file storage are hosted in the data centers of our cloud infrastructure providers.

3. **Authentication and Push Notification Providers:**

4. **Crash Reporting and Telemetry Providers:**
   * **Google Firebase Crashlytics:** Collects anonymized technical stack traces and system diagnostics to help us fix bugs.

5. **Transactional Email Services:**
   * Dedicated enterprise SMTP providers deliver login links and essential security notifications.

## 9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

### 9.1. How to Delete Your Account
* **Inside the App:** Go to `Settings > Delete My Account`. A warning screen is shown and you must type `DELETE` to confirm.
* **By Email:** If you cannot access the app, email **support@orti.app** from your registered address or follow the steps at [orti.app/delete-account](https://orti.app/delete-account). Requests are completed within 30 days.

**Account deletion is irreversible.** There is no waiting or restore period.

### 9.2. What Happens to Your Data
**Data that is deleted:**
* Your email address, phone number, profile photo, username, and authentication records,
* Records in your personal wallet that belong only to you, and files attached to them,
* Your sessions and push notification tokens.

**Data that remains in groups:**
Groups are a shared ledger, and they are required to keep other members' past balances and settlements accurate. Therefore:
* Expenses, amounts, descriptions, notes, and receipt images you added to groups **continue to be kept as the group's record**.
* Your account remains in groups as a **"ghost" member** that holds no email, phone, or other contact data. Only the display name shown in the group is kept.
* A group admin can rename this member (for example, to a nickname).
* These records are kept until the group itself is deleted.
* You can request removal of a specific receipt or of your name entirely at **support@orti.app**.

**If you later want to rejoin a group:** A group admin matches your email address to the ghost member, and when you sign in with a verified email, your previous membership is linked to you. The deleted account itself cannot be restored.

**Backups and security logs:** Deleted data is purged from backups on a rolling basis within a reasonable period. Server logs kept for security and abuse prevention are retained for a limited time.

### 9.3. Exporting Your Data
You can request a copy of your data at **support@orti.app**. We are working on an in-app export feature you can use before deleting your account; this policy will be updated when it becomes available.
